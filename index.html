01 — Geographic Distribution

Where are threatened species found?

Establish the geographic pattern and reveal differences between states and taxonomic groups.

GEOGRAPHIC PATTERN

Unique Species Richness by Jurisdiction

New South Wales records the highest standardized species count, making it the central hotspot for national conservation priority. Northern Territory exhibit the lowest standardized values, reflecting fewer total recorded listings under current state frameworks.

TAXONOMIC DISTRIBUTION

Different groups dominate across regions

Amphibians show tight coastal clustering along eastern catchments, heavily impacted by freshwater degradation. Mammals and Birds display broader range fragmentation, relying on isolated predator-free reserves and southern island refugia.

THREAT STATUS DISTRIBUTION

Threatened species cluster unevenly across Australia

Critically Endangered and Endangered species heavily cluster along the southeastern coastline and Southwest WA, representing acute extinction risks. Vulnerable and Near Threatened species are more broadly distributed across regional and inland corridors, indicating widespread ecosystem vulnerability.

02 — THREAT COMPOSITION AND BIODIVERSITY STRUCTURE

Which species and groups are most affected?

Move beyond locations to explore threat categories, taxonomic relationships and species distribution.

THREAT STATUS COMPOSITION

Mix of threat levels varies across states

Critically Endangered and Endangered categories make up over 40% to 60% of total listings in most states. Vulnerable species represent the single largest remaining share across jurisdictions, highlighting a broad pipeline of fauna and flora requiring proactive protection.

THREATENED PLANTS VS ANIMALS

Plants and animals show different patterns across states

New South Wales and Western Australia record the largest botanical listing burdens, driven by acute habitat loss and high levels of regional plant endemics. Territories like the Northern and Australia Capital display significantly smaller overall counts, though animals slightly outpace or equal plant listings in northern tropical ecosystems.

TAXONOMIC HIERARCHY

Threatened species span a complex taxonomic hierarchy

Concentric Sunburst Rings map the hierarchy from inner Kingdom down to outer Genus level, where segment arc length directly reflects relative species count. Plants exhibit heavy concentration in a few massive classes, whereas animal species spread across a wider variety of distinct families.
