AUSTRALIAN BIODIVERSITY

Australia's Threatened Species

One of the world's 17 megadiverse countries, hosting between 600,000 and 700,000 native species—roughly 7% to 10% of all species on Earth.

WHAT ARE THEY?

THREAT STATUS COMPOSITION

Mix of threat levels varies across states

The 100% stacked bars show the proportion of threat categories for each state. While total species counts vary, states like New South Wales show larger relative shares of Critically Endangered species, revealing distinct threat composition profiles.

KINGDOM COMPOSITION

Plants and animals show different patterns across states

Plants and animals are not evenly represented across Australia. Some states show a stronger concentration of threatened plant species, while others have relatively more threatened animal species, revealing distinct biodiversity profiles across all states.

TAXONOMIC STRUCTURE

Threatened species span a complex taxonomic hierarchy

The sunburst reveals how Australia's threatened species are organised from Kingdom through Class, Family, and Genus. The expanding branches show that threatened biodiversity is distributed across many taxonomic groups, with some families and genera containing substantially more species than others.

TAXONOMIC HIERARCHY

Explore how threatened species are organised across taxonomic levels

SPECIES DISTRIBUTION

Species with higher threat levels may have more restricted distributions

The beeswarm plot compares conservation status with the number of states where each species is recorded. Species found in fewer states may have more restricted distributions, while the spread of dots reveals differences across birds, mammals, amphibians and plants. These patterns help highlight potential geographic vulnerability among Australia's species.

02 — Time Series

WHAT IS HAPPENING OVER TIME?

Threatened species are not distributed evenly across Australia. This map shows the number of unique threatened species recorded within each Australian state and territory.

POPULATION CHANGE

Percentage Change in Threatened Species Abundance

This chart shows the percentage change in combined species abundance relative to 2000. Only taxonomic groups with valid abundance data in 2000 are included, ensuring that percentage changes can be calculated consistently. Values below 0% indicate a decline from the 2000 baseline, while positive values indicate an increase.

THREAT STATUS CHANGE ACROSS YEAR

Changes in abundance differ across threat levels

Comparing 2000 with 2021 reveals different levels of change across IUCN statuses. Categories with longer connections experienced larger percentage shifts in combined abundance, while shorter connections indicate more limited change over time.

STATE RANKING

The relative position of states changes over time

The bump chart ranks states according to the combined abundance of threatened species in each year from 2000 to 2021. Changes in the lines show which states moved higher or lower in the ranking, highlighting shifts in their relative position over time.
